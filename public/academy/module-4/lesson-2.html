Scripts de Negociación (Palabra por Palabra)

📖 9 min lectura 🎓 Módulo 4 - Lección 2 ⭐ Scripts Probados

La importancia de tener scripts

Problemas comunes
  • "No sé qué decir cuando el broker me ofrece un rate bajo"
  • "Me quedo sin palabras y termino aceptando"
  • "Tengo miedo de sonar unprofessional"

Solución: scripts probados. No tienes que improvisar. Usa frameworks que funcionan.

Script #1: "The Value Sandwich"

Estructura:

  1. Acknowledge (reconoce la oferta)
  2. Value (establece tu valor)
  3. Ask (pide lo que necesitas)

Ejemplo completo:

BROKER: "Load pays $1,200 for 750 miles"

YOU [Acknowledge + Value + Ask]: "Thanks for thinking of me on this, John. I see it's a hot load with tight delivery window, and you know I'm always reliable on timing. For rush service and the distance, I'd need $1,400 to make it work. Can we do that?"

BROKER: "Best I can do is $1,280"

YOU: "I appreciate that. Let me do $1,320 and I'll make sure it's there 2 hours early. Deal?"

BROKER: "OK, $1,320 works. Thanks."

+10%

$1,200 → $1,320 negociado en 60 segundos.

Script #2: "The History Play"

Cuándo usar: cuando ya tienes una relación establecida con el broker.

BROKER: "I have a load, $900 for 600 miles"

YOU: "Hey Sarah! I'd love to help. Quick question - we've done what, 6-7 loads together now? And I've always taken care of you, right?"

BROKER: "Yeah, you've been great"

YOU: "I really appreciate that. This route, with the deadhead I have, I'd normally need $1,050 to make my numbers work. Since it's you, can we meet at $1,020?"

BROKER: "Let me see... OK, $1,020 works"

YOU: "Perfect, you got it. Picking up in 2 hours"

Key: Aprovecha tu historial sin sonar pesado.

Script #3: "The Alternative Mention"

Cuándo usar: cuando tienes otra opción (pero prefieres este broker).

BROKER: "Load is $1,100, that's my best"

YOU: "I appreciate you calling me first. Full transparency - I do have another offer for similar miles at $1,250. But I prefer working with you because you're always straight with me. Any chance you can get to $1,200? That would make it worth passing on the other one."

BROKER [opciones]: A) "Let me call customer, hold on" · B) "I can do $1,180" · C) "Can't move on this one"

YOU [si B]: "You know what, I'll do $1,180. Let's keep the relationship good" — [si C]: "I totally understand. Keep me in mind for the next one"

Key: Menciona la alternativa, pero deja claro que prefieres trabajar con ellos.

Script #4: "The Timing Leverage"

Cuándo usar: la carga es urgente y el broker necesita cubrirla YA.

BROKER: "I need this covered ASAP, pickup in 3 hours. Pays $850 for 500 miles"

YOU: "I can help you out - I'm only 20 minutes from the pickup, so I can be there before anyone else. For the rush and quick turnaround, my rate would be $1,050. Worth it to have it covered in the next 30 minutes?"

BROKER: "That's high... can you do $975?"

YOU: "You know what, because it's you and I know this is urgent, I'll do $1,000 even. But I need confirmation right now so I can start heading there."

BROKER: "Done. Sending paperwork now"

Key: La urgencia es tu ventaja. Úsala con profesionalismo.

Script #5: "The Partnership Approach"

Cuándo usar: cuando buscas construir una relación a largo plazo.

BROKER: "I have 3-4 loads weekly on this lane. Looking for a reliable carrier. Rate is $1.15/mile"

YOU: "That's interesting - I run this lane often. Let me ask you this: what if we structured it differently? If I commit to being your go-to for this lane, taking at least 2-3 loads per week consistently, could we do $1.30/mile? I'm thinking long-term partnership here, not just one-off loads."

BROKER: "Interesting... let me run numbers. I could potentially do $1.25/mile if you're truly committed to volume"

YOU: "That works. Let's shake on it - I'll be your dedicated carrier for this lane. When's the first load?"

Key: El compromiso de volumen justifica una tarifa premium.

Script #6: "The Soft Decline"

Cuándo usar: la tarifa es demasiado baja pero quieres mantener la relación.

BROKER: "Best and final is $950"

YOU: "I really appreciate you working with me on this. Unfortunately $950 doesn't quite work with my costs on this particular run. But I definitely want to work with you. Do you have anything else coming up this week that might be a better fit?"

BROKER: "Let me check... actually I have one going opposite direction tomorrow"

YOU: "Perfect! Tell me about that one"

Key: Rechaza ESTA carga, pero mantén la puerta abierta.

Script #7: "The Respectful Hard No"

Cuándo usar: la tarifa es imposible y el broker insiste.

BROKER: "That's my final offer, $800 for 650 miles"

YOU: "I respect that you have your numbers to hit. Unfortunately at $800, I'd actually lose money on this run after fuel and all costs. I'm not able to work for free, but I really do want to work with you in the future. Please keep me in mind when you have loads that are in the $1.20-1.40/mile range. I'll always answer your calls."

BROKER: "Fair enough. I'll reach out next time"

Key: Rechazo profesional + la puerta se mantiene abierta.

Counter-offers: la danza de números

Reglas de counter-offers
  1. Nunca contraofertes más de 2 veces. Primero: pide $1,400. Contraoferta 1: cede a $1,320. Contraoferta 2: cierra en $1,280. PARA AHÍ. Si no llegan a un acuerdo, rechaza la carga.
  2. Muévete en incrementos razonables. ❌ Mal: $1,500 → $1,495 → $1,490. ✅ Bien: $1,500 → $1,400 → $1,320.
  3. Cada contraoferta debe llevar una concesión. "I'll do $1,320 AND deliver 2 hours early" / "I'll do $1,280 if you cover tolls".
  4. Conoce tu número límite ANTES de contestar. Antes de llamar: "My minimum is $1,250". No te desvíes de ese número durante la negociación.

El "power pause"

Silencio estratégico:

Ejemplo

BROKER: "Best I can do is $1,150"

YOU: [5 segundos de silencio]

BROKER: "...OK let me see if I can get $1,200"

Por qué funciona:

  • El silencio incomoda
  • El broker llena el silencio con una concesión
  • Demuestra que lo estás considerando en serio
  • No pareces desesperado por aceptar de inmediato

No abuses: 1-2 veces por negociación MAX.
