Frequently Asked Questions

Is this rate per mile calculator free?

Yes, it is 100% free and needs no sign-up. It calculates your real RPM, deadhead included, right in your browser.

What is the difference between gross RPM and real RPM?

Gross RPM divides the pay by the loaded miles only — the number you see on the load board. Real RPM divides the same pay by ALL the miles you drive, including the deadhead to the pickup. Real RPM is always lower, and it is the only number that reflects what you really earn.

What are deadhead miles?

They are the miles you drive without a paid load — from where you are to the pickup. Nobody pays you for them, but they burn fuel, wear and time, so they must be part of your real RPM. Read the full deadhead guide →

What does this basic calculator NOT include?

It does not include your real fixed and variable costs (insurance, maintenance, fuel for your van), repositioning miles to get out of dead zones, or net profit.
