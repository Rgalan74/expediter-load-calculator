Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous experience as an expediter?

No. The course is designed to take you from zero to operating profitably — Module 1 itself starts from the fundamentals, without assuming you already know how to calculate your numbers.

How long do I have access to the content?

For life, as long as your plan stays active. If you cancel your subscription, you always keep access to the Tour, Start Here, and Modules 1-3 for free; your previous tier's content (Pro/Premium) locks until you reactivate.

What if I have questions while taking the course?

You can write directly by email — there's no forum or formal chat yet, but every question gets a personal reply.

Can I change plans or ask for a refund?

Academy access follows your Smart Load Solution subscription plan — it's not billed separately. You can change plans anytime from your settings panel; the change is immediate. See full details on plans and pricing.
