Module 7 · Lesson 1

More income without driving more

8 min read Growth Premium
What you will learn
  1. Real options to add income.
  2. The legal requirements you cannot skip.
  3. How to compare your hour in each option.

01The driving ceiling

When you drive, your income has a limit: the hours in the day and the miles in the week. To grow without burning out, some drivers add sources that do not depend on being behind the wheel. Not all of them fit everyone, and none should neglect the main business.

02Real options

Passing loads to other carriersGetting paid for loads you cannot haul. Doing it legally requires broker authority with FMCSA and a $75,000 bond. Without that, do not charge for loads you do not move yourself
Direct customersLocal companies that often ship urgent freight. Fewer middlemen, but you do the selling and the collecting
Special servicesCareful loading and unloading, exact-appointment deliveries, handling delicate equipment. Charged separately (Extra charges to customer)
Teaching other driversHelping new ones get started: costs, authority, first brokers. Your experience has value
Before starting any of them

Check the legal and insurance requirements of each option. An authority or insurance mistake can cost you more than everything you make with it.

03Is it worth your hour?

Compare what each hour of the new source leaves you with what an hour of driving leaves you. If your profit per day is $265 and you drive 10 hours, your hour is worth about $26.50.

04One at a time

  • Start with a single source and give it six months.
  • Track its income and expenses separately to know whether it works.
  • If it costs you good loads or rest, it is not worth it.

In summary

  • Driving has a ceiling: hours and miles.
  • Every extra source has legal and insurance requirements.
  • Compare it with your profit per driving hour.
