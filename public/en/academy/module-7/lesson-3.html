Module 7 · Lesson 3

Your personal brand

8 min read Growth Premium
What you will learn
  1. Why a simple brand gets you more loads.
  2. How to write your introduction line.
  3. What proof of your service you can show.

01Why a brand helps you

For a broker who sees hundreds of carriers, most are an MC number. A simple, consistent brand makes them remember you and think of you when the load you do well comes up.

02Your introduction line

[What you do] in [area or niche]. [Time] of experience. [What sets you apart].
Example

"Detroit-based cargo van, urgent freight to the Midwest and Southeast. Four years without a late delivery. I answer within minutes, day and night."

03What the broker sees

Your vanClean inside and out, with your name and MC number visible
Your email and signatureAn email with your business name, MC, phone and the areas you cover
Your carrier packetW-9, certificate of insurance and authority ready as PDFs to send within minutes
Your online presenceA professional profile and real photos of your equipment. Little, but up to date

04Proof of what you say

  • Your record: how many loads you hauled and how many on time. You get it from your History.
  • Recommendations: ask your best brokers for a line about your service.
  • Training: if you have niche certifications, show them.
No exaggerating

Never put numbers you cannot prove. A broker who catches an exaggeration does not call again.

In summary

  • A simple brand makes them remember you.
  • Have your line and your carrier packet ready.
  • Show only what you can prove with your record.
