Module 7 · Lesson 5

Building a business with value

9 min read Growth Premium
What you will learn
  1. The difference between a job and a business.
  2. What gives your business value.
  3. How to start today with the app.

01Owner, not just driver

If the business only works while you drive, what you have is a job. If it works with systems, clean numbers and customers who do not depend only on you, you have something that can grow, be handed over or be sold.

02What gives a business value

Clean numbersComplete loads and expenses, month by month. The app's P&L statement and tax report give you the base
Spread-out customersNo broker with too much weight. You see it in the Company report
Written systemsHow you quote, accept and collect. Something someone else can follow
Transferable relationshipsBrokers who know your company, not just your cell phone
Provable recordYears in operation, on-time loads, no claims
Equipment in good shapeMaintenance recorded and up to date

03How it is valued

Small businesses are usually valued as a multiple of what they leave the owner in a year. The multiple depends a lot on the industry, the risk and everything above: with clean numbers and spread-out customers it is worth more. For a real figure, talk to an accountant or business advisor.

What lowers value

Depending on a single broker, incomplete numbers, everything in your head and neglected equipment.

04Start today

  • Record every load and every expense in the app, no exceptions.
  • Write one page on how you quote and accept a load.
  • Check every quarter that no broker weighs too much.
  • Keep the monthly reports in a folder per year.

In summary

  • A business with value runs on systems, numbers and spread-out customers.
  • Your app reports are the base of your numbers.
  • For a real valuation, talk to a professional.

Module 7 complete

You now know how to evaluate extra income sources, pick a niche with your numbers, present yourself as a professional, build your network and give your business value.
