Module 7 · Lesson 4

Networking in the industry

7 min read Growth Premium
What you will learn
  1. Who makes up your network.
  2. How to build it with little time.
  3. How to use market notes to remember contacts.

01Why your network matters

Many of the best loads never show up on the load board: they come through a call from someone who knows you. Your network is the people who think of you when an opportunity comes up.

02Five circles

Brokers and dispatchersThe direct source of loads
Shippers and receiversThe dock people: they know what ships and when
Other driversThey pass loads, zone tips and contacts
VendorsTrusted mechanics, insurance agents and accountants
Industry groupsOnline groups, trade shows and associations

03How to build it

  • Help first. Pass a load you cannot take to someone you trust; it comes back.
  • Be friendly at every dock. Receiving staff remember whoever treats them well.
  • Save every contact with name, company and where you met.
  • Follow up: a short message a few days after meeting someone.
  • Contribute in groups with useful information, not just asking for loads.

04Use the app to remember

The market notes in Zones also work for your network: "Atlanta, GA: ask for Ana at receiving, dock 4". When you quote a load there again, the note shows up in Keep in mind.

Ten minutes a week

A message to a broker, a hello to a contact and one new note. Little, but steady.

In summary

  • The best loads often come through someone who knows you.
  • Help first and follow up.
  • Keep your contacts and notes in Zones.
