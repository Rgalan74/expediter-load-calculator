Module 7 · Lesson 2

Niches that pay more

9 min read Growth Premium
What you will learn
  1. Which niches exist and what each requires.
  2. How to choose yours.
  3. How to measure with the app whether it really pays more.

01Specializing

Some loads pay more because few carriers can or want to haul them: they require training, equipment, more insurance or availability at any hour. Specializing in one takes you out of competing on price alone.

02Common cargo van niches

Medical and pharmaceuticalSamples, equipment and medicine. They usually require privacy (HIPAA) and bloodborne pathogens training, and temperature control
High-value electronics and equipmentServers, prototypes, broadcast gear. They require higher cargo insurance and chain of custody
Aerospace (AOG)Parts for grounded aircraft. Total urgency and 24/7 availability
AutomotiveParts for stopped production lines. Repeat lanes between plants
Art and white gloveCareful handling, packing and appointment delivery
Hazardous materialsThey require training, paperwork and, depending on type and quantity, permits and endorsements. Check FMCSA and PHMSA rules before accepting a single load

03How to choose

  • Is there demand in your area? Look at which special loads your brokers already offer you.
  • What does it cost to get in? Training, equipment and the insurance difference.
  • Does it fit your life? AOG and medical ask you to be available at any hour.
  • Does it really pay more? Measure it with your numbers, not with what social media says.

04Measure it with the app

Rates in each niche change with the market, so we do not quote numbers from memory. Run the test:

  • Type the niche in Company or in the load number, for example "MED - Acme".
  • After 10 or 20 loads, compare RPM and profit per load in the Company report and in History.
  • Add what you pay extra (insurance, equipment) to your costs in Settings before comparing.
Gradual transition

Keep your regular freight while you do the first niche loads. Shift the weight of your week only when the numbers confirm it.

In summary

  • Niches pay more because they demand more: training, equipment, insurance or availability.
  • Choose by demand, entry cost and how you want to live.
  • Measure with your numbers before changing your week.
