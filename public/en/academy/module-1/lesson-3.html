Break-even: your survival number

8 min read Fundamentals Free
What you will learn
  1. What break-even is and why it is your minimum.
  2. How to go from break-even to your target with a margin.
  3. How the app uses it to tell you accept, evaluate or reject.

01What break-even is

Break-even is the minimum you need to charge per mile to not lose money. It is your real cost per mile, the one you calculated in lesson 2.

Below break-evenyou lose money
Right at break-evenyou work for free
Above break-evenyou make money

02With the lesson 2 example

The example van has a real cost of $0.534 per mile. That is its break-even. Three 500-mile loads:

At $0.45 per mile−$42
At $0.534 per mile$0
At $0.95 per mile+$208

03The "something is better than nothing" trap

The common mistake

"I have nothing; this load pays $0.45 per mile, at least it is something." With a $0.534 break-even you lose $0.084 per mile: over 600 miles that is $50 out of your own pocket to work that day.

Often it is better to wait for a load that pays or to reposition toward a better zone.

04Break-even is not your target

Break-even is your survival minimum. Your target has to leave you a profit margin:

Target = break-even ÷ (1 − margin)
Target with a $0.534 break-even
20% margin$0.668 /mi
30% margin$0.763 /mi
40% margin$0.890 /mi
Watch out

It is not the same as multiplying by 1.30. With a 30% margin, out of every $100 you charge you keep $30 in profit. If you multiply your cost by 1.30, your real margin is only 23%.

That margin is your real profit, your cushion for slow months and emergencies, and the money to grow the business.

05Miles change your break-even

With the example fixed costs ($1,910 a month) and lodging ($400 a month) spread over the miles:

More miles that pay lower your break-even and make you more competitive. More miles below break-even only make you lose more.

07How the app uses it

Apply it in the app

In Settings you enter your target profit margin and, if you want, a minimum RPM. With your cost per mile, the app calculates each load's target and tells you:

  • ACCEPT if the load reaches your target.
  • EVALUATE if it covers your costs but does not reach the target.
  • REJECT if it does not cover your costs.

Open Settings →

In summary

  • Break-even is your real cost per mile: below it, you lose money.
  • Your target is break-even ÷ (1 − margin), not break-even × 1.30.
  • In the app, the decision and the suggested price come from your cost and your margin.
