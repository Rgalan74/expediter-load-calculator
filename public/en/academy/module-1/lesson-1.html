What Real RPM Is (and Why 90% Calculate It Wrong)

📖 6 min read 🎓 Module 1 - Lesson 1

The most common mistake

Most expediters calculate RPM like this:

Total Pay ÷ Loaded Miles = RPM
This is wrong

Why? Because you're ignoring the miles you don't get paid for but that still cost you money.

The correct formula

REAL RPM = Total Pay ÷ (Deadhead + Loaded + Repositioning)

Where:

  • Deadhead = Miles from your position to pickup
  • Loaded = Miles you get paid for (pickup → delivery)
  • Repositioning = Miles from delivery to your next freight zone

Full example

Situation:

  • Your position: Toledo, OH
  • Pickup: Detroit, MI (65 miles deadhead)
  • Delivery: Laredo, TX (1,000 loaded miles)
  • Offered pay: $1,500
Wrong calculation #1 (how 70% do it) $1.50/mile
Wrong calculation #2 (how 25% do it) $1.41/mile
Correct calculation (only the top 5%) $1.12/mile

The real breakdown:

  • Deadhead (Toledo → Detroit): 65 miles
  • Loaded miles (Detroit → Laredo): 1,000 miles
  • Repositioning (Laredo → San Antonio): 280 miles
  • Total REAL miles: 1,345 miles
REAL RPM: $1,500 ÷ 1,345 = $1.12/mile

Impact analysis

Wrong calculation $1.50/mile
Correct calculation $1.12/mile
$511

in imaginary earnings over 1,345 miles — a $0.38/mile (34%) error in your RPM calculation.

If your break-even is $1.10/mile, you think you're making $0.40/mile when you're actually only making $0.02/mile.
