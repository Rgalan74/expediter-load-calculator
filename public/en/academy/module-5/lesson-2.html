Module 5 · Lesson 2

The KPIs that matter

9 min read Finance Pro
What you will learn
  1. What each Finances card means.
  2. Three indicators you calculate yourself.
  3. The early warning signs.

01What a KPI is

A KPI is a number that tells you whether you are on track before it is too late. You do not need to follow twenty: seven in the app and three you calculate yourself are enough.

02The seven in Finances

Average RPMIncome ÷ total miles. Compare it with your ACCEPT threshold$0.96
Cost per mileExpenses ÷ miles. It includes food, which the calculator does not count (it is personal): take it out before comparing with Settings$0.59
MarginProfit ÷ income. Compare it with your target margin39.0%
Income, expenses, profit and milesThe size of the month. Compare them with last month
Compare yourself with yourself

There is no "good" RPM or margin for everyone. What matters is your RPM against your threshold, your real cost against your configured one and your margin against your target.

03Three you calculate

Profit per day workedMonthly profit ÷ days you worked. The panel gives it per load; here you see it for the month
Empty-mile percentageEmpty miles ÷ total miles. If it rises, review your zones
Share of your biggest brokerIn the Company report. If one goes above 30 or 40%, you depend on them

04Early warnings

  • The Finances cost per mile rises two months in a row.
  • Average RPM falls below your ACCEPT threshold.
  • Margin falls below your target margin.
  • You drive fewer miles than your minimum number (lesson 1).
Ask Lex

"How am I doing this month?" or "compare me with last month". Lex answers with your Finances numbers.

In summary

  • Compare RPM with your threshold, real cost with the configured one and margin with your target.
  • Add profit per day, empty percentage and the share of your biggest broker.
  • Act when an indicator gets worse two months in a row.
