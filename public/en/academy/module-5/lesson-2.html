KPIs That Predict Success or Failure

📖 15 min read 🎓 Module 5 - Lesson 2 📈 KPIs

What is a KPI?

KPI = Key Performance Indicator

In plain terms: numbers that tell you whether you're winning or losing BEFORE it's too late.

Analogy: KPIs are like vital signs in a hospital.

  • Blood pressure
  • Heart rate
  • Temperature
  • Blood oxygen

If one is off, you need intervention NOW. Your business is no different.

The 10 essential KPIs

KPI #1: Real RPM (Revenue Per Mile)

Total Revenue ÷ Total Miles (including deadhead) $10,500 ÷ 7,200 mi = $1.46/mi
  • 🟢 $1.60+/mi = Excellent
  • 🟡 $1.40-1.60/mi = Good
  • 🟠 $1.20-1.40/mi = Average
  • 🔴 <$1.20/mi = Problem

Why it matters: your #1 rate efficiency metric.

KPI #2: Profit Margin

(Net Profit ÷ Gross Revenue) × 100 ($4,200 ÷ $10,500) × 100 = 40%
  • 🟢 40%+ = Excellent
  • 🟡 30-40% = Good
  • 🟠 20-30% = Marginal
  • 🔴 <20% = Danger

Why it matters: what you ACTUALLY keep.

KPI #3: Cost Per Mile (CPM)

Total Expenses ÷ Total Miles $6,300 ÷ 7,200 = $0.875/mi
  • 🟢 <$0.85/mi = Excellent efficiency
  • 🟡 $0.85-1.00/mi = Good
  • 🟠 $1.00-1.15/mi = High
  • 🔴 >$1.15/mi = Serious problem

Why it matters: if CPM rises, expenses are out of control.

KPI #4: Deadhead Ratio

(Deadhead Miles ÷ Total Miles) × 100 (950 ÷ 7,200) × 100 = 13.2%
  • 🟢 0-10% = Excellent
  • 🟡 10-15% = Acceptable
  • 🟠 15-20% = Improvable
  • 🔴 >20% = Losing money

Why it matters: unpaid miles = waste.

KPI #5: Van Utilization

(Loaded Miles ÷ Available Miles) × 100
Available Miles = Days worked × 500 mi/day
5,400 ÷ (22 × 500) = 49%
  • 🟢 60%+ = Excellent
  • 🟡 50-60% = Good
  • 🟠 40-50% = Improvable
  • 🔴 <40% = Too much downtime

Why it matters: how much time your van is generating revenue.

KPI #6: Profit Per Day Worked

Monthly Net Profit ÷ Days Worked $4,200 ÷ 22 = $191/day
  • 🟢 $200+/day = Excellent
  • 🟡 $150-200/day = Good
  • 🟠 $100-150/day = Surviving
  • 🔴 <$100/day = Not sustainable

Why it matters: your return for each day of effort.

KPI #7: Load Acceptance Rate

(Loads Accepted ÷ Loads Offered) × 100 (28 ÷ 45) × 100 = 62%
  • 🟢 50-70% = Excellent (selective but accessible)
  • 🟡 40-50% = Good
  • 🟠 70-85% = Accepting too much
  • 🔴 <40% or >85% = Problem

Why it matters: sweet spot, not too high, not too low.

KPI #8-10: other indicators

  • #8 - RPM by zone: identifies profitable zones vs ones to avoid
  • #9 - Time between loads: monitors positioning efficiency
  • #10 - ROI: is this business worth it vs other investments?

Early warning dashboard

Red alert — immediate action
  • Margin <25% for 2+ months
  • CPM >$1.10/mi
  • Deadhead >20%
  • Utilization <40%
  • RPM declining 3 consecutive months

Yellow alert (monitor):

  • Margin 25-30%
  • CPM $0.95-1.10/mi
  • Deadhead 15-20%
  • Utilization 40-50%
All good
  • Margin >35%
  • CPM <$0.90/mi
  • Deadhead <12%
  • Utilization >55%
  • RPM improving

Rule: 2+ red alerts = crisis, intervene now. 3+ yellow = negative trend, adjust. All green = stay the course.

Key takeaway

The 10 essential KPIs:

  1. Real RPM (rate efficiency)
  2. Profit Margin (true profitability)
  3. CPM (expense control)
  4. Deadhead Ratio (route efficiency)
  5. Utilization (van productivity)
  6. Profit/Day (return on effort)
  7. Acceptance Rate (selectivity balance)
  8. RPM by Zone (where to earn more)
  9. Time Between Loads (operational efficiency)
  10. ROI (is the business worth it?)
Golden rule

Review KPIs weekly. 10-15 minutes every Sunday. Problems caught early are easy to fix. Problems ignored for months can sink your business.

Your business is only as healthy as your KPIs say it is. Don't guess. MEASURE.

KPIs aren't about impressing anyone. They're your early warning system.
