The core takeaway

Route analysis consistently reveals:

  • 20% of your routes generate 60% of all profit (Tier S/A).
  • 50% of your routes are just "okay" (Tier B).
  • 30% of your routes are actively destroying profitability (Tier C/D).

The metrics that matter most, per route:

  1. Profit Per Hour (the #1 metric, always)
  2. Net Margin (measures business sustainability)
  3. RPM (measures rate efficiency)
  4. Backhaul Rate (measures load continuity)
  5. Load Frequency (measures operational consistency)
The optimization process

Weeks 1-2: identify your unprofitable bad routes. Weeks 3-6: test replacement routes strategically. Weeks 7-10: gradually replace the bad with the good. Weeks 11-12: evaluate your total financial results.

Typical real-world outcome: +30-50% increase in monthly net profit.

Not all routes are created equal. The secret is not working more hours — it is working the right routes.

Eliminate 2-3 of your worst routes. Maximize 2-3 of your best routes. = Transform your business completely in 90 days.
