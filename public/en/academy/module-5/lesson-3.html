01Not every mile is worth the same

Your average RPM hides big differences. In the example account the average is $0.96, but loads leaving Michigan pay $1.30 and loads leaving Florida $0.50, below cost.

03Classify your routes

KeepRPM above your threshold. Look for them and look after those brokers
NegotiateThey cover cost but not your target. Ask for the counter-offer or use them only to reposition
AvoidBelow your cost. Only if they get you out of a bad zone and you know what you lose

04Build your circuit with data

  • Once a month, open Zone profitability and write down your 5 best routes.
  • Look for loads that connect those routes.
  • Talk to the brokers who give you those routes (module 4).
  • For the red routes, save a market note with the reason.

In summary

  • The average hides very good routes and routes that lose money.
  • Zones and the Finances reports tell you which ones.
  • Keep the green ones, negotiate the yellow ones and avoid the red ones.
