When to think about a second van

9 min read Finance Pro
What you will learn
  1. The signs you are ready to grow.
  2. How to run the numbers on a second van.
  3. The most common mistakes when expanding.

01The usual question

"If I make good money with one van, I will make double with two." Not always. With a second van you pay a driver, another insurance policy and another vehicle, and your RPM has to cover it all.

02Signs you are ready

  • You have had stable profit for at least 6 months and know your real cost per mile.
  • You are offered more good loads than you can take.
  • You have savings for 3 to 6 months of the second van's fixed costs.
  • Your loads and expenses are in order in the app: without that you will not know whether the second van wins or loses.

The lesson

At a $0.96 RPM, the second van loses about $240 a month. You need at least $0.99 just to break even, and quite a bit more to be worth the risk. First raise your RPM and cut your cost with one van.

04What usually goes wrong

  • Expanding before you know your real cost.
  • Hiring without a clear agreement on pay, hours and vehicle use.
  • Not counting the extra insurance, maintenance and weeks without a driver.
  • Neglecting your own loads to manage the second van.
Before a second van

Raise your RPM with preferred brokers, cut your empty miles and have six months of clean numbers. If the math works with that, a second van makes sense.

In summary

  • A second van needs an RPM that covers vehicle and driver.
  • Have 6 months of clean numbers and savings before expanding.
  • First raise your RPM and cut your cost with one van.
