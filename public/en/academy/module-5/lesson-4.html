Planning the month: scenarios

8 min read Finance Pro
What you will learn
  1. The formula to plan your month.
  2. How profit changes with RPM, empty miles, fuel and miles.
  3. How to measure the month as it goes.

01One formula is enough

You do not need a complicated model to plan the month. With three numbers you get a good idea of what is coming:

Monthly profit = miles × (RPM − cost per mile)

With 8,000 miles, a $0.96 RPM and a $0.59 cost: 8,000 × $0.37 = $2,960.

Where the money is

Ten cents more RPM is worth more than almost any saving. That is why negotiating well is the biggest lever.

04Measure the month as it goes

  • Every Friday: check in Finances how much you have and how many miles are left to reach your number.
  • Mid-month: if you are behind, prioritize long loads in your circuit.
  • Seasons: note which months were slow this year to plan the next one.
