Beyond "How Much Did I Make": Real Financial Tracking

📖 12 min read 🎓 Module 5 - Lesson 1 📊 Analysis

The income illusion

Common scenario: Driver A says "I made $12,000 this month!" Driver B says "I only made $9,000." Who is doing better? Answer: it depends.

Revenue$12,000
Miles9,500

Profit$3,900
Margin32.5%
Driver B
Revenue$9,000
Miles6,200
Fuel$2,400
Maintenance$550
Other expenses$1,850
Profit$4,200
Margin46.7%
The reason: efficiency

Driver B earns LESS but takes home MORE.

The 3 layers of numbers

Layer 1: gross revenue (what you invoice)

  • Total load payments
  • FSC (Fuel Surcharge)
  • Detention pay
  • Bonuses/incentives
Example: $10,500/month

Layer 2: operating expenses (what you pay)

Variable:

  • Fuel, DEF, Tolls
  • Meals on the road
  • Preventive maintenance

Fixed:

  • Van payment, Insurance
  • Licenses/plates
  • Subscriptions, Phone
Example: $6,800/month

Layer 3: net profit (what you actually earn)

Revenue - Expenses: $10,500 - $6,800
$3,700

net profit for the month — a 35.2% margin.

The common mistake

MOST operators only look at Layer 1. YOU need to master all 3.

The simple tracking system

Absolute minimum — record daily:

  1. Revenue today: Load #1: $XXX, Load #2: $XXX, Total: $XXX
  2. Miles today: Deadhead: XXX, Loaded: XXX, Total: XXX
  3. Expenses today: Fuel: $XXX (XX gallons), Tolls: $XXX, Meals: $XXX
  4. Notes: Anything unusual
Time required: 3-5 minutes/day

Benefit: complete clarity on your business.

Setting your "minimum number"

Monthly fixed expenses ÷ Target margin (35-40%) = Minimum revenue required

Example:

Fixed expenses: $2,200/month ÷ 0.40 (40% target margin) = $5,500 minimum revenue

This means:

  • You need to invoice at least $5,500/month
  • To cover fixed costs and generate profit
  • Below this = losing money
  • Above this = building your business
Your minimum monthly number

Memorize it. If you see you're tracking below it mid-month, you know you need more loads now.

Key takeaway

"How much did I make?" is the wrong question.

Wrong question

"How much did I make this month?"

Right questions
  • How much did I INVOICE? (gross revenue)
  • How much did I SPEND? (total expenses)
  • How much did I KEEP? (net profit)
  • What was my MARGIN? (%)
  • How does it COMPARE to previous months? (trend)

The 3 numbers you must always know:

  1. Your monthly minimum number (break-even with a healthy margin)
  2. Your target margin (35-45% is healthy in expediting)
  3. Your trend (improving or declining?)

One month without tracking is one month flying blind.

Financial tracking is not complicated — it's 3-5 minutes of logging per day. The difference between doing it and not is the difference between guessing whether you made money and KNOWING your exact profitability.
