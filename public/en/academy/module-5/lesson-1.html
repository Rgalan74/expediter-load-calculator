Module 5 · Lesson 1

Beyond "how much did I make"

8 min read Finance Pro
What you will learn
  1. The difference between income and real profit.
  2. How your expenses break down per mile.
  3. How to calculate how many miles you need per month.

01The income illusion

"I billed $5,066 this month" sounds good. But what you pay at home comes from what is left after all expenses. There are three different numbers and you should not mix them up:

IncomeEverything you got paid for your loads$5,066
ExpensesFuel, vehicle, insurance, permits, maintenance, food, hotel, tolls$3,090
Real profitWhat you actually keep: 39% of income$1,976

Watch low-mileage months

The van payment, insurance and permits are fixed: you pay them even if you do not drive. If you drive fewer miles in a month, your cost per mile goes up.

03A simple system

  • Every load: Accept and save in the calculator. Income adds up by itself.
  • Every expense: Finances → Add expense, with its category, the same day.
  • Every week: check the Finances summary.
  • Every month: compare the Finances cost per mile, without food, with the one in Settings and adjust.

04Your minimum number

Before judging whether a month was good, you need to know how much you have to make. Add your fixed business costs and what you need for your household, and divide by what you keep per mile.

In summary

  • What you pay at home comes from real profit, not income.
  • Record every load and every expense in the app the same day.
  • Calculate your minimum miles per month.
