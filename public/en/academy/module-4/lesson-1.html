Module 4 · Lesson 1

Negotiation fundamentals in expediting

8 min read Negotiation Pro
What you will learn
  1. What the broker needs and how to use it in your favor.
  2. The three kinds of power: urgency, relationship and alternatives.
  3. The three numbers you need before answering.

01Negotiating is not fighting

In expediting the broker needs the same thing you do: the load delivered on time with no problems. A good negotiation ends with a rate that works for you and a broker who wants to call you again.

What the broker needsCover the load fast, a carrier who does not cancel, on-time delivery and updates along the way
What the broker does not wantCancellations, renegotiating mid-trip, going silent
Your advantage

On urgent loads, being fast and reliable is worth as much as price. That is what gives you the power to ask for more.

02Three kinds of power

UrgencyHigh: the load is urgent, the broker called late, you are the only one nearby. Low: a "whenever" load, many vans in the area
RelationshipHigh: you have hauled several loads for them without problems and they call you directly. Low: first time
AlternativesHigh: you have another offer or a good week. Low: you have been sitting two days in a dead zone

With high power, you can ask for more and hold it. With low power, do not show you are in a hurry and focus on not going below your number.

03Your three numbers before you answer

Do not negotiate without numbers. The calculator's Suggested price block gives them to you for every route. Detroit → Atlanta, 752 miles, $480 offer:

04What a professional looks like

  • Answer fast, even if it is to say no.
  • Talk about miles, hours and zone, not about what you "need".
  • If you accept, deliver everything you said.
  • If you decline, leave the door open for the next one.

In summary

  • Good negotiation closes a rate that works and keeps the broker.
  • Measure your power: urgency, relationship and alternatives.
  • Ask for the Counter-offer, accept from the Target, never below Break-even.
