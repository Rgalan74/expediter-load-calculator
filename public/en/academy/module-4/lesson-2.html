📝 THE IMPORTANCE OF SCRIPTS

Common problems:

  • "I don't know what to say when a broker offers a low rate"
  • "I freeze up on the phone and just end up accepting"
  • "I'm terrified of sounding unprofessional or desperate"

THE SOLUTION: Proven Scripts.
You don't have to improvise. Use communication frameworks that actually work.

🎭 SCRIPT #1: "THE VALUE SANDWICH"

Structure:

  1. Acknowledge (recognize their offer respectfully)
  2. Value (clearly establish your unique value)
  3. Ask (directly ask for the number you need)
Complete Example:

BROKER: "This load pays $1,200 for 750 miles"

YOU: "Thanks for thinking of me on this, John.
[ACKNOWLEDGE]

I see it's a hot load with a tight delivery window,
and you know I'm always reliable on timing.
[VALUE]

For rush service and the distance, I'd need
$1,400 to make it work. Can we do that?"
[ASK]

BROKER: "Best I can do is $1,280"

YOU: "I appreciate that. Let me do $1,320 and
I'll make sure it's there 2 hours early.
Do we have a deal?"

BROKER: "OK, $1,320 works. Thanks."

Result: $1,200 → $1,320 (+10% in exactly 60 seconds)

🎯 SCRIPT #2: "THE HISTORY PLAY"

When to use: When you have an established relationship with the broker.

BROKER: "I have a load, $900 for 600 miles"

YOU: "Hey Sarah! I'd love to help you out.
Quick question - we've done what, 6-7 loads
together now? And I've always taken care
of you, right?"

BROKER: "Yeah, you've been great"

YOU: "I really appreciate that. This route,
with the deadhead back that I have, I'd normally
need $1,050 to make my numbers work.
Since it's you, can we meet at $1,020?"

BROKER: "Let me see... OK, $1,020 works"

YOU: "Perfect, you got it. I'll be picking up in 2 hours"

Key: Leverage your history without being pushy or entitled.

💪 SCRIPT #3: "THE ALTERNATIVE MENTION"

When to use: When you genuinely have another option (but you prefer this broker).

BROKER: "Load is $1,100, that's my absolute best"

YOU: "I appreciate you calling me first.
Full transparency - I do have another
offer for similar miles sitting at $1,250.

But I prefer working with you because
you're always straight with me.

Any chance you can get to $1,200?
That would make it worth me passing on
the other one."

BROKER: [Typical Options]
A) "Let me call the customer, hold on"
B) "I can do $1,180"
C) "I literally can't move on this one"

YOU: [If B] "You know what, I'll do $1,180.
Let's keep the relationship good"
[If C] "I totally understand. Keep me
in mind for the next one."

Key: Mention your alternative but explicitly show preference for them.

⏰ SCRIPT #4: "THE TIMING LEVERAGE"

When to use: The load is highly urgent, and the broker needs cover NOW.

BROKER: "I need this covered ASAP, pickup in 3 hours.
Pays $850 for 500 miles"

YOU: "I can absolutely help you out - I'm only 20 minutes
from the pickup, so I can be there before
anyone else even gets on the highway.

For the rush and quick turnaround, my rate
would be $1,050. Worth it to have it securely covered
in the next 30 minutes?"

BROKER: "That's pretty high... can you do $975?"

YOU: "You know what, because it's you and I know
this is an urgent headache, I'll do $1,000 even.
But I need confirmation right now so I can
start heading there immediately."

BROKER: "Done. Sending paperwork now."

Key: Urgency is your biggest advantage. Use it professionally.

🤝 SCRIPT #5: "THE PARTNERSHIP APPROACH"

When to use: When attempting to build a long-term dedicated relationship.

BROKER: "I have 3-4 loads weekly on this lane.
Looking for a reliable carrier.
Rate is $1.15/mile"

YOU: "That's interesting - I run this lane often.
Let me ask you this:

What if we structured it differently?
If I fully commit to being your go-to for this lane,
taking at least 2-3 loads per week consistently,
could we lock it in at $1.30/mile?

I'm thinking long-term partnership here,
not just cherry-picking one-off loads."

BROKER: "Interesting... let me run the numbers.
I could potentially do $1.25/mile
if you're truly committed to that volume"

YOU: "That works. Let's shake on it - I'll be
your dedicated carrier for this lane.
When's the first load ready?"

Key: Volume commitment is the ultimate justification for a rate premium.

📞 SCRIPT #6: "THE SOFT DECLINE"

When to use: Rate is way too low, but you really want to keep the relationship alive.

BROKER: "Best and final is $950"

YOU: "I really appreciate you working with me
on this. Unfortunately $950 doesn't quite
work with my costs on this particular run.

But I definitely want to work with you.
Do you have anything else coming up
this week that might be a better fit?"

BROKER: "Let me check... actually I have one
going the opposite direction tomorrow"

YOU: "Perfect! Tell me about that one."

Key: Decline THIS specific load, but aggressively keep the door open.

🚫 SCRIPT #7: "THE RESPECTFUL HARD NO"

When to use: Rate is financially impossible and the broker is insisting.

BROKER: "That's my final offer, $800 for 650 miles"

YOU: "I completely respect that you have your numbers to hit.
Unfortunately at $800, I'd actually lose money
on this run after fuel and my fixed costs.

I'm not exactly able to work for free, but I really
do want to work with you in the near future.

Please keep me in mind when you have loads
that are solidly in the $1.20-1.40/mile range.
I'll always answer your calls."

BROKER: "Fair enough. I'll reach out next time."

Key: A highly professional decline ensures the door stays open.

💬 COUNTER-OFFERS: THE DANCE OF NUMBERS

THE IRON RULES OF COUNTER-OFFERS:

1. Never counter more than 2 times
First: Ask for $1,400
Counter 1: Meet at $1,320
Counter 2: Final at $1,280
STOP THERE. If you can't reach an agreement, decline gracefully.

2. Move in reasonable, logical increments
❌ BAD: $1,500 → $1,495 → $1,490 (You look petty)
✅ GOOD: $1,500 → $1,400 → $1,320 (You look serious)

3. Every counter MUST have a concession attached
"I'll come down to $1,320 AND I'll deliver 2 hours early"
"I'll do $1,280 IF you cover all the tolls"

4. Know your exact walk-away number BEFORE you answer
Before answering the call: "My absolute minimum is $1,250"
Do not blindly deviate from your number during the tension of the negotiation.

🎯 THE "POWER PAUSE"

The psychological weight of strategic silence:

BROKER: "Best I can do is $1,150"

YOU: [5 full seconds of dead silence]

BROKER: "...OK let me see if I can get $1,200"

Why it works flawlessly:

  • Silence makes people extremely uncomfortable.
  • Brokers naturally fill the silence with a concession.
  • It mathematically shows you're considering the offer seriously.
  • It proves you are not desperate to accept immediately.

WARNING: Only use this 1-2 times per negotiation MAX.
