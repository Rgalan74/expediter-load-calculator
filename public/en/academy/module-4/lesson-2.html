Module 4 · Lesson 2

Negotiation scripts, word for word

9 min read Negotiation Pro
What you will learn
  1. Six ready-to-use scripts for brokers.
  2. When to use each one.
  3. The counter-offer rules.

01Why have scripts ready

Most drivers accept the first offer because they do not know what to say. With a few prepared lines you do not have to improvise.

The example

Detroit → Atlanta, 752 miles. The broker offers $480. Your panel says: Counter-offer $559, Target $517, Break-even $414.

02Six scripts

1. Acknowledge, add value, ask

For almost any offer.

What you say

"Thanks for thinking of me. I'm 30 miles from the pickup and I can be loaded today. For 752 miles I need $559 to make it work. Can you do that?"

2. Use your history

When you have already hauled several loads for them.

What you say

"We've done several loads together and they've all delivered on time. On this lane with my empty miles I need $559. Can you get closer to that?"

3. Mention an alternative (only if true)

When you really do have another offer.

What you say

"I'd rather work with you, but I have another offer for similar miles that pays more. If you can do $540, it's yours."

4. Use urgency

When the broker needs it covered now.

What you say

"I'm 20 minutes from the pickup and can roll right now. For a same-day pickup my rate is $575. Send the rate confirmation and I'm on my way."

5. Propose a regular lane

When the broker has frequent loads on the same lane.

What you say

"I run this lane often. If you have it every week, I can be your regular truck on it. What rate could you do for a steady commitment?"

6. Decline with the door open

When the rate does not work and you want to keep the broker.

What you say

"I appreciate it, but $480 doesn't cover my costs on this run. Keep me in mind for the next one, especially anything heading north."

03Counter-offer rules

  • Two counter-offers at most. $559, then $535, then you close or walk away.
  • Reasonable steps. From $559 to $535 makes sense; from $559 to $557 looks like a game.
  • Every step down in exchange for something. "I'll do $535 with quick pay" or "if the delivery appointment is early tomorrow".
  • Your minimum does not move during the call. If it does not reach your Target, decline with the door open.

04Silence

After you say your number, stop talking. Whoever speaks first usually gives in. If the broker says "that's a lot", ask: "What can you do?" and go back to your numbers.

Ask Lex for help

With the load in the calculator, ask Lex: "give me a line to ask for the counter-offer". It uses the panel figures and your notes for that place.

In summary

  • Use prepared lines: acknowledge, add value and ask for a number.
  • Two counter-offers at most, and every step down in exchange for something.
  • If it does not reach your Target, decline with the door open.
