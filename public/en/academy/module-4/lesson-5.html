Module 4 · Lesson 5

Long-term relationships

8 min read Negotiation Pro
What you will learn
  1. How your relationships evolve from year one to year three.
  2. How to keep a relationship without asking for a load.
  3. How to avoid depending on a single broker.

01The long game

Many new carriers do not make it past their first or second year. It is rarely for lack of work or bad driving: it is from depending on the load board and a few brokers with no relationship. What you build in the first two years decides the next ones.

02A realistic timeline

Months 1 to 3 · learnYou work with many brokers. You note who pays on time and who is professional
Months 4 to 6 · chooseYou focus on the best 10 to 15. You start declining the bad ones
Months 7 to 12 · buildSeveral brokers call you regularly. You negotiate with more confidence
Year 2 · consolidateYou turn the good ones into preferred and add a few new brokers, on purpose
Year 3 and beyond · choose your loadsYou have a reputation on your lanes and can be selective even in slow season

03Keeping the relationship

  • A call every three months to your best brokers, not only when you need a load.
  • Share your plans: lanes you will run, weeks you will be off.
  • If you slip, tell them first. A delay you warn about is forgiven; a hidden one is not.
  • Say thanks for the good loads and the emergencies they passed you.
Quarterly call

"Hey, just checking in. Anything I can do better on my side? I'll be running the Midwest a lot next month."

04Do not depend on just one

The Company report shows what percentage of your income comes from each broker. If a single one goes above 30 or 40%, you depend on them: if they change jobs, lower their rates or close, your week falls apart.

Check it every quarter

Open Finances → Reports → Company report and look at the percentage column.

Open Finances →

In summary

  • What you build in two years decides the next ones.
  • Call your best brokers even when you do not need a load.
  • Check in the Company report that no single one weighs too much.

Module 4 complete

You now know how to negotiate with your numbers, what to say word for word, how to become the preferred carrier, when to say no and how to look after your relationships over time.
