Module 4 · Lesson 4

Red flags and when to say no

9 min read Negotiation Pro
What you will learn
  1. Caution signs and danger signs.
  2. How to verify a broker.
  3. How to track payments in Finances → Accounts.

01Caution

Vague details"Somewhere in the Chicago area", "flexible", "general freight". Ask for address, delivery window, weight and commodity in writing
The rate keeps changingMonday $1,200, Tuesday $1,150. Ask for a firm rate before you commit
Pressure to decide nowA real emergency still gives you a few minutes to run numbers. If not, let them move on
What you say

"I need a firm rate and the full details in writing before I commit."

02Danger: stop

They ask you for money"Setup fee", "deposit", "dispatch fee". A legitimate broker pays you, never the other way around. It is a scam
You cannot verify their MCLook it up on FMCSA SAFER: active broker authority and a current $75,000 bond
No rate confirmationNo written rate confirmation, no load. Never move freight on a verbal agreement only
Payment details changeA different company, a different account or a factoring company you do not know. Confirm by calling the broker's official number

03Slow payers

A broker who pays late costs you money even if the rate is good. The app marks every saved load as pending payment, with a payment date 15 days out. In Finances → Accounts you see by company what is paid, pending or overdue.

Rule of thumb

If a broker has overdue loads with you, do not accept another one until they pay. If it happens again, write a note and drop them.

04How to say no

Saying no in time protects your money, your time and your reputation. Keep it short and professional:

What you say

"Thanks, but this one doesn't work for me. Keep me in mind for the next one."

No exceptions

If they ask you for money or you cannot verify the MC, there is no negotiation. Decline and move on.

In summary

  • Vague details, a changing rate or pressure: get everything in writing.
  • If they ask for money or you cannot verify the MC, decline.
  • Track your payments in Accounts and do not repeat with slow payers.
