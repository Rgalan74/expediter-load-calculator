Module 4 · Lesson 3

Relationships: brokers who call you first

9 min read Negotiation Pro
What you will learn
  1. The three levels of a broker relationship.
  2. What makes you a preferred carrier.
  3. How to measure each broker with Finances and History.

01Three levels

Level 1 · Load boardYou search. You compete with many vans for the same load and the rate drops
Level 2 · They know youSome brokers message you when they have something in your area
Level 3 · PreferredThey call you first. You compete with few or nobody and can hold your rate

The goal is to have 5 to 10 brokers at level 3.

02What makes you preferred

  • Reliability. You do not cancel after confirming. You arrive on time or warn ahead.
  • Communication. You update at loading, halfway and at delivery, with photos and the BOL.
  • Speed. You answer within minutes and pick up fast.
  • Consistency. You are regularly available on the same lanes.

03How it is built, load by load

Load 1 · first impressionConfirm 30 minutes before arriving, send a photo at loading, an update halfway and the BOL at delivery
Loads 2 to 5 · consistencyThe same standard every time. The broker starts to know what to expect
Loads 6 to 10 · a bit extraNow and then cover an emergency or deliver early. Not always, or it becomes expected
Message at loading

"Loaded and rolling, ETA 6 pm. I'll send an update halfway."

04Measure each broker with the app

Always type the Company in the calculator. With that, the app tells you which broker really works for you:

Finances → Reports → Company reportIncome, share of total, loads, miles, RPM and dollars per load for each broker
HistorySort by company or export to Excel to see every load
Finances → AccountsWhich loads were paid, which are pending and which are overdue, by company
What you are looking for

A broker who gives you many loads at a low RPM can be worth less than one who gives you few but well-paid loads and pays on time. Look at RPM and payments, not just volume.

In summary

  • The goal is 5 to 10 brokers who call you first.
  • Reliability, communication, speed and consistency.
  • Type the Company on every load to measure each broker.
