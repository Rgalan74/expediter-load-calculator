Reality

Every fixed expense is negotiable. The difference is 30 minutes of calls. The ROI is infinite.

Insurance: your biggest fixed expense

Typical cargo van expediting
Liability$300-500/month
Cargo$200-300/month
Physical damage$150-250/month
Uninsured motorist$50-80/month
Total$700-1,130/month (average $880/month)

But this is highly optimizable.

Strategy: aggressive shopping (every 12 months)

Step 1: get quotes from a minimum of 5 companies.

Commercial specialists:

  • Progressive Commercial, Hartford, Nationwide, State Farm, Travelers

Trucking specialists:

  • CoverWallet, OOIDA, Commercial Insurance Solutions, NTIS
Time: 3-4 hours for 5 quotes
Potential savings: $150-250/month ($1,800-3,000/year)

Step 2: negotiate with your current provider.

"I've been a customer for [X years]. I've shopped around and received
lower offers:
- Company B: $760/month
- Company C: $695/month

My current rate is $880/month. I like working with you, but I can't
justify paying $185/month more.

Can you match or come close to $695/month?"

Don't be afraid to switch. The process takes 2-3 days. It's worth every minute.

Step 3: coverage optimization.

  • Physical damage on an older van: if worth less than $15,000, consider liability + cargo only (savings: $150-180/month)
  • Higher deductible: $500 → $2,500 = $55/month savings
  • Verify broker requirements before reducing coverage

Process
  1. List all your subscriptions
  2. Ask: "Did I use this in the last 30 days?"
  3. If no → cancel immediately
  4. If yes → "Is there a cheaper alternative?"

Negotiating with mechanics

Tactic #1: long-term relationship

"I'm a commercial cargo van operator. I drive 90,000 miles/year. I'm looking for a shop for ALL my maintenance. Can you offer a volume discount?"

Result: 10-15% labor discount + 5-10% parts discount = $40-80/month savings.

Tactic #2: buy your own parts

Example — brakes
Shop provides partsLabor $180 + parts $220 = $400
You provide partsLabor $180 + parts $110 (online) = $290
Savings$110 (27.5%)

Buy from: RockAuto.com, Amazon, AutoZone, O'Reilly. Not all shops accept this — ask about their policy first.

Tactic #3: get multiple shop quotes

For jobs over $500: get 3+ shop quotes. Price difference can run 20-40%. Annual savings: $300-600.
