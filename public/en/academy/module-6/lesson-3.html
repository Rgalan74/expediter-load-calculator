Module 6 · Lesson 3

Cutting your fixed costs

8 min read Operations Pro
What you will learn
  1. Why fixed costs weigh so much.
  2. How to review insurance and costs that pile up.
  3. How to reflect each saving in the app.

01What you pay even if you do not drive

Vehicle payment, insurance, permits, phone and subscriptions. They are fixed: you pay them with or without loads. Every dollar you cut here raises your profit every month, without driving one more mile.

02Insurance

  • Get quotes every year before renewing, from at least three agents who work with carriers.
  • Check the deductible. A higher one lowers the premium, but you need that money saved.
  • Your record counts. With no accidents or violations, ask for it to be reflected in the price.
  • Ask about annual or semi-annual payment. Sometimes it is cheaper than monthly.

03What piles up without you noticing

SubscriptionsApps, load boards, streaming, storage. Check your statement and cancel what you did not use last month
Phone and internetCompare plans every year; you often pay for data you do not use
ShopsA trusted mechanic who knows you charges fairly and sees you fast when you need it
MembershipsRoadside assistance or fuel discounts: worth it if you use them, not for having them
