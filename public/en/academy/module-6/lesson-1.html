⛽ THE TRUTH ABOUT FUEL

REALITY: Fuel is your #1 expense. Typically 28-32% of gross revenue.

In a cargo van (17-20 MPG):

  • 7,500 miles/month
  • ÷ 18.5 MPG average = 405 gallons/month
  • × $3.50/gal = $1,418/month in fuel

BUT... With optimization:

  • Gain 2 MPG (18.5 → 20.5) = -30 gal/month
  • Better prices ($3.50 → $3.35) = -$61/month
  • TOTAL SAVINGS: $166/month ($2,000/year)

🎯 THE 10 EFFICIENCY TACTICS

TACTIC #2: MPG-Focused Maintenance

  • Clean air filter: Dirty = -10% MPG (Savings: $40/month)
  • Properly inflated tires: -5 PSI = -3% MPG (Savings: $30-45/month)
  • Wheel alignment: Misaligned = -5% MPG (Savings: $60/month)
  • Low-rolling-resistance tires: +8.3% MPG (Savings: $110/month)
  • Synthetic oil: +1-2% MPG (Savings: $15-25/month)

COMBINED SAVINGS: $100-150/month

TACTIC #3: Purchase Strategy

4 LAYERS OF SAVINGS:

  1. Chain memberships (Pilot, Loves, Speedway): $0.08-0.12/gal = $36/month
  2. Cash back apps (GetUpside, GasBuddy): 5-25¢/gal = $20-35/month
  3. Strategic timing (Monday-Wednesday, not weekends): $15-25/month
  4. Route planning (Fill up in cheaper states): $42/month

TOTAL SAVINGS: $100-140/month

TACTIC #4: Idle Reduction

Idle burns 0.5-0.8 gallons/hour = $1.75-2.80/hour wasted

  • Waiting at pickup/delivery: 45 min/day = $49/month
  • Lunches with engine running: 30 min/day = $32/month
  • Warming/cooling van: 15 min/day = $18/month
  • TOTAL WASTE: $99/month

SIMPLE RULE: If you'll be stopped >3 minutes, SHUT IT OFF
SAVINGS: $60-80/month

TACTICS #5-10: Additional Optimizations

  • #5 Weight and load: Remove 125 lbs of unnecessary gear = $15-25/month
  • #6 Aerodynamics: Close windows, remove roof rack = $20-30/month
  • #7 Anticipation: Maintain momentum, brake less = $25-40/month
  • #8 Strategic A/C: <45 mph windows open, >45 mph A/C = $30-50/month
  • #9 Smart GPS: Avoid traffic/idle = $15-25/month
  • #10 Morning fuel: Denser in the morning = $5-10/month
