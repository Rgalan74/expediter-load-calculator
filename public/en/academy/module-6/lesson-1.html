Module 6 · Lesson 1

Fuel: your biggest variable cost

9 min read Operations Pro
What you will learn
  1. How to calculate your fuel cost per mile.
  2. How to measure your real MPG and what improves it.
  3. How to enter your per-gallon discount in the app.

01What each mile of fuel costs you

Fuel cost per mile = price per gallon ÷ MPG

At $3.70 a gallon and 19 MPG: $0.195 per mile. Over 8,000 miles a month that is about $1,558. That is why every MPG you gain shows.

02Measure your real MPG

  • Fill the tank and write down the odometer.
  • Next time, fill up again and write down gallons and miles.
  • MPG = miles driven ÷ gallons pumped. Average it over several weeks.
  • Enter that number in Settings → MPG (your vehicle).

03What moves your MPG the most

SpeedMore speed means more air resistance and more fuel. Going from 70 to 62 mph usually shows in your MPG and barely changes arrival time on a one-day trip
Smooth drivingHard acceleration and braking burn fuel. Anticipate traffic and use cruise control on flat highway
IdlingWaiting with the engine running also burns fuel. Turn it off on long waits when the weather allows
Tire pressureLow tires burn more fuel and wear faster. Check them cold every week
Weight and roofDo not carry what you do not need and avoid accessories that add drag

04Pay less per gallon

If your fuel card or discount app gets you, say, 20 cents below average, type -0.20 in Your price vs. average. The calculator applies it to every load.
