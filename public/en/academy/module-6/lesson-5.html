Module 6 · Lesson 5

Continuous improvement: one tweak a week

7 min read Operations Pro
What you will learn
  1. The plan, try, measure and decide cycle.
  2. Concrete ideas to start.
  3. Which numbers to check every month.

01Small and steady

You do not need to change everything at once. A small tweak every week, measured and kept, adds up to a lot in a year. The idea is simple: plan, try, measure and keep what works.

02The cycle

1 · PlanPick one thing: "I will drive 62 mph on the highway"
2 · TryDo it for two weeks, without changing anything else
3 · MeasureCompare the number you wanted to move: MPG, cost per mile, RPM or empty miles
4 · DecideIf it worked, it stays as a habit. If not, drop it and try something else

03Ideas to start

  • Ask for the counter-offer on every load that says EVALUATE.
  • Lower your cruising speed and measure MPG.
  • Decline loads with more than 25% empty miles unless the pay covers it.
  • Call a preferred broker every week without asking for a load.
  • Cancel a subscription you do not use.
  • Write a market note for every problem on the road.

04Your monthly review

Cost per mileFinances. Did it drop versus last month?
Average RPMFinances. Is it above your ACCEPT threshold?
MarginFinances. Does it reach your target?
Green statesZones. Did your circuit grow?
Ask Lex

"Compare me with last month." Lex tells you what improved and what got worse with your data.

In summary

  • One tweak at a time, for two weeks.
  • Measure the number you wanted to move before deciding.
  • Check cost per mile, RPM, margin and green states every month.
