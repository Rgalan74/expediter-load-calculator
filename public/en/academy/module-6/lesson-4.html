Module 6 · Lesson 4

Systems: less paperwork, more control

8 min read Operations Pro
What you will learn
  1. The routine for every load, from offer to payment.
  2. How to handle receipts and expenses without losing anything.
  3. What the app does by itself and what is on you.

01Your time costs too

Every hour spent looking for a receipt or redoing the math is an hour you do not drive or rest. A simple system, always the same, gives you those hours back and avoids costly mistakes.

02The routine for every load

When you get the offerRun it through the calculator (or paste it into Lex) before answering
When you accept itAccept and save, with Company and Load #
At deliveryPhoto of the signed BOL, sent to the broker the same day
When you get paidFinances → Accounts → Mark paid

03Receipts and expenses

  • Scan every receipt with your phone the same day (apps like Adobe Scan or Microsoft Lens).
  • Keep them in a cloud folder per month.
  • Record the expense in Finances → Add expense, with its category.

04What the app already does for you

Monthly income and milesThey add up by themselves with every saved load
Expected payment dateEvery load is pending with a date 15 days out; Accounts shows pending and overdue
ReportsP&L statement, company report, expense breakdown and zone profitability, ready to print
TaxesThe Schedule C aligned report (Premium plan) organizes income and expenses for your accountant
History in ExcelExport your loads with their costs whenever you need

05The Sunday review

  • Are all of the week's loads saved?
  • Are all expenses recorded?
  • Any overdue loads in Accounts? Email or call that Monday.
  • Did you learn something about a place? Save it as a market note.
Fifteen minutes

This review takes about fifteen minutes and saves you the end-of-month and tax-season chaos.

In summary

  • Every load: calculate, save, BOL, mark paid.
  • Every receipt: scan and record the same day.
  • Every Sunday: a fifteen-minute review.
