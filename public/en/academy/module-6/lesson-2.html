Module 6 · Lesson 2

Preventive maintenance

8 min read Operations Pro
What you will learn
  1. What a breakdown on the road costs.
  2. A practical maintenance calendar.
  3. How the per-mile reserves in Settings work.

01The maintenance math

Preventive maintenance is cheap and planned. A breakdown on the road is expensive and comes at the worst time: towing, urgent repairs and days without work.

Preventive
Oil and filters on timeplanned
Brake and tire checksplanned
Days lost0
Breakdown on the road
Towing and urgent repairsurprise
Lost or reassigned loadreputation
2 days × $265 a day-$530

02Your calendar

Every dayQuick walk-around: lights, tires, leaks, fluid levels
Every weekCold tire pressure, wipers, brake fluid
Per the manualOil and filters, tire rotation, brakes, transmission. Use the manufacturer's severe-service intervals: expediting is severe service
Every seasonBattery and coolant before winter; air conditioning before summer

03Set the money aside per mile

Settings has three per-mile reserves: Maintenance, Tires and Repairs. The calculator charges them on every load, so the money for the next service is already in your price.

Maintenance$0.014/mi
Tires$0.012/mi
Repairs$0.025/mi
Total · over 8,000 miles$408 a month
Make it real

Move that amount every month into a separate account. When you do the service, record it in Finances → Add expense → Maintenance. If your real costs exceed the reserve, raise it in Settings.

04You or the shop

You can do itCheck fluids and pressure, change wipers, bulbs and air filter
Better at the shopBrakes, suspension, transmission, electrical. A mistake there costs more than the saving

05Signs you do not ignore

  • Check engine or brake light on.
  • New noises when braking or turning.
  • Steering wheel vibration at highway speed.
  • Engine temperature higher than normal.
  • Burning smell or leaks under the van.

In summary

  • A breakdown costs days of work, not just the repair.
  • Follow the manufacturer's severe-service intervals.
  • Per-mile reserves put maintenance inside your price.
