Interpreting the Result: Accept, Negotiate, or Decline

📖 8 min read 🎓 Module 2 - Lesson 3

🎯 THE 3 POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

After running the numbers on a load, there are really only three paths you can take:

✅ ACCEPT → The load meets or exceeds your targets. Lock it in!
⚠️ NEGOTIATE → The load is close, but needs a little push to make it work.
❌ DECLINE → The load simply isn't profitable. Walk away.

✅ WHEN TO ACCEPT IMMEDIATELY

The "Green Light" Criteria - AUTO-ACCEPT if ALL of these are true:

  • ☐ Profit margin > 25%
  • ☐ Real RPM > $1.40/mile
  • ☐ Deadhead ratio < 15%
  • ☐ Delivery is in a strong freight zone
  • ☐ Hourly rate > $40/hr

If it checks all 5 boxes, don't overthink it. ACCEPT.

⚠️ WHEN TO NEGOTIATE

The Negotiation Zone:

  • Profit margin: 15-25% (Borderline)
  • Real RPM: $1.20 - $1.40/mile
  • Or there's a specific negative factor that could easily be offset by a higher payout.

💬 POWERFUL NEGOTIATION SCRIPTS

Script #1: High Deadhead

"Hey, the load pays well, but I've got about 150 miles of deadhead to get to you. To make the numbers work on my end, I'd need to be closer to $1,250 instead of $1,100. Can we make that happen?"

Script #2: Weak Delivery Zone

"I see this delivers right into [Dead Zone]. It typically takes me 48 hours just to find a reload out of there. Is there any flexibility on the rate? I was thinking $1,350 rather than $1,200."

Script #3: Multiple Stops

"I noticed this run has 3 stops. That easily adds 2-3 hours to my day. My base rate would be $1,300, but with the extra stops, I'd need $1,450. Can we work with that number?"

THE SECRET SAUCE:

Always justify your counter-offer with a specific, logical reason. Never just say "I want more money."

❌ WHEN TO WALK AWAY WITHOUT NEGOTIATING

Absolute Red Flags - INSTANT DECLINE if:

  • ☐ Profit margin < 10% or negative
  • ☐ Real RPM < $1.10/mile
  • ☐ Deadhead ratio > 25%
  • ☐ Delivery in a dead zone with zero added compensation
  • ☐ Load requires gear or equipment you don't carry
  • ☐ Broker seems shady or untrustworthy

There is no point negotiating these. Politely DECLINE and move on.

📊 THE COMPLETE DECISION MATRIX

Profit Margin Real RPM Your Action
> 30% > $1.50 ✅ ACCEPT INSTANTLY
25-30% $1.40 - 1.50 ✅ ACCEPT
20-25% $1.30 - 1.40 ⚠️ ACCEPT/NEGOTIATE
15-20% $1.20 - 1.30 ⚠️ NEGOTIATE HARD
10-15% $1.10 - 1.20 ❌ LIKELY DECLINE
< 10% < $1.10 ❌ INSTANT DECLINE

🧠 ADDITIONAL FACTORS TO CONSIDER

1. Context and Timing

The exact same load hits differently depending on the context:

  • Monday 9 AM: A load giving a 20% margin → Wait for something better ⚠️
  • Friday 5 PM: A load giving a 20% margin → Accept it! ✅ (The weekend is starting soon)

2. Your Financial Reality

  • Having a phenomenal week (already at $2,000 profit) → Be picky and selective.
  • Having a miserable week (only $600 profit) → You might need to take borderline loads.
  • End of the month (bills are due) → Be more flexible.

HOWEVER: Never accept a load that activelyLOSES you money. EVER.

3. Market Conditions

  • Saturated Market (Too many vans): Margins get tighter. A 20-22% margin might actually be "great."
  • Tight Market (Lots of freight): You have the leverage. Demand premium rates (30-35% margins are reachable).

💡 THE KEY TAKEAWAY

Not every load deserves the same energy or treatment:

  • ✅ Truly great loads → Accept in under 30 seconds
  • ⚠️ Borderline loads → Negotiate with total confidence
  • ❌ Terrible loads → Decline without a single ounce of regret

Your time is your most precious asset. Don't waste it trying to negotiate the impossible.

Trust your numbers. If the app shows a RED alert, it almost always means RED.
