Module 2 · Lesson 6

Lex: your decision copilot

7 min read Practice Free
What you will learn
  1. What to ask Lex and which data it uses.
  2. How to paste the text of an offer to fill in the calculator.
  3. How to read its verdicts and what its limits are.

01What Lex is

Lex is the app assistant. You open it with the chat button. Unlike a generic chatbot, it answers with your data: your cost per mile, your target margin, your saved loads, your notes and, when available, the weather at the destination.

02What to ask

The load on your screen"Should I take it?", "analyze it". Lex uses the panel numbers: you do not have to repeat them
A state or zone"How have I done in Ohio?" It answers with your loads saved there
Your month"How am I doing this month?" It uses what you have in Finances
Saving a noteIn Spanish, Lex recognizes "guarda una nota para Laredo: poca carga de salida" and saves it for that place

03Paste the text of an offer

Copy the offer text from the load board or the broker message and paste it in the chat. Lex reads the rate, the miles and, if it is there, the deadhead, and puts them in the calculator.

Check two things

The RPM Lex shows in this message is rate ÷ loaded miles. The panel RPM uses total miles, empty miles included. And type or check the origin and destination in the calculator: without a route, the panel does not calculate.

04How to read its answers

When it analyzes a load, Lex ends with one of these verdicts and shows you the numbers it used:

ACCEPTRPM reaches your threshold
ALMOST ACCEPT / EVALUATE CAREFULLYIt covers your cost, but the margin is thin: negotiate
REJECTRPM is below your cost: you would lose money

For the load on your screen, Lex follows the panel verdict. If there is snow, ice or a storm at the destination, it warns you.

05Three real uses

1 · Should I take it?

With Detroit → Nashville for $900 on screen, you ask "should I take it?". Lex gives you the RPM ($1.63), your cost, the profit (about $596) and its verdict: ACCEPT.

2 · Pasted offer

You paste "Load to Atlanta: $1,350 for 722 miles, DH 30mi". Lex fills in the calculator and asks whether to analyze it.

3 · Your notes

Weeks ago you saved a note for Laredo: little outbound freight. When you analyze another load into Laredo, Lex reminds you.

06Limits

  • Lex has a daily message limit depending on your plan. Use it for decisions, not for chatting.
  • Its answers about your business depend on you saving your loads and expenses.
  • Lex helps you decide faster. The final decision is yours.

In summary

  • Lex answers with your costs, your loads and your notes.
  • Paste an offer and Lex fills in miles, rate and deadhead.
  • For the load on screen, Lex follows the panel verdict.

Module 2 complete

You now know how to fill in the calculator without mistakes, count empty miles, read the verdict, negotiate with the suggested price and use Lex to decide.
