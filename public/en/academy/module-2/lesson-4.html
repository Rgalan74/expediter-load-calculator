7 mistakes when filling in the calculator

8 min read Practice Free
What you will learn
  1. The mistakes that make a bad load look good.
  2. What goes in Tolls, Extra charges and Total rate.
  3. A quick list to check before deciding.

01Mistake 1: Leaving empty miles at 0

Nashville → Laredo, 1,094 miles for $850. With no empty miles, the panel says ACCEPT: RPM $0.78 and $248 profit. But there is little outbound freight in Laredo and you have to go to San Antonio: 157 more miles.

With those miles in Empty miles, the panel changes to EVALUATE: RPM $0.68, $162 profit and about $54 per day.

How to avoid it

Always ask yourself: "is there freight where I deliver?". If not, add the repositioning to the empty miles.

02Mistake 2: Typing only the city

"Chicago" can be downtown, O'Hare or Joliet. Between them there are dozens of miles that change the RPM.

How to avoid it

Type city and state and pick the suggestion from the list. If you have the exact address, use it.

03Mistake 3: Mixing up tolls and extra charges

Putting an extra payment from the broker in Tolls subtracts it instead of adding it. And putting tolls in Extra charges counts them as income.

How to avoid it

Tolls = what you pay on the road. Extra charges to customer = what you get paid separately (waiting, unloading, detention or toll reimbursement).

04Mistake 4: Leaving out part of the pay

The broker says "$1,200 plus $150 fuel surcharge". If you type $1,200, the panel shows a worse load than it really is.

How to avoid it

Total rate is everything you get paid to move the load, fuel surcharge included.

05Mistake 5: Outdated Settings

Your insurance went up, your vehicle payment changed or you drive fewer miles a month. If Settings does not know, your cost per mile is lower in the app than in real life. Miles per month matter: your fixed costs are spread across them.

How to avoid it

Check Settings once a month. The fuel price updates by itself for each route; your MPG and fixed costs do not.

06Mistake 6: Not saving the loads you haul

If you calculate, accept and close, the load does not exist for the app. History, Zones, Finances and Lex are left without your real data.

How to avoid it

When you accept a load, use Accept and save.

07Mistake 7: Looking only at RPM

A short load can have a $3 RPM and leave you $100 for half a day. A long one into a zone with no freight can have a good RPM and leave you few dollars per day.

How to avoid it

Also look at the net profit and the profit per day the panel shows you.

08Checklist before calculating

  • Origin and destination with state, picked from the list.
  • Empty miles to the pickup, plus repositioning if you deliver into a zone with no freight.
  • Total rate with everything you get paid for the load.
  • Tolls as an expense; separate payments from the broker in Extra charges to customer.
  • Settings checked this month.
Do it now

Open Settings and check your fixed costs, your MPG and your miles per month. It takes five minutes.

Open Settings →

In summary

  • Add repositioning to empty miles when you deliver into a zone with no freight.
  • Tolls is an expense; what you get paid separately goes in Extra charges.
  • Check Settings every month and save the loads you haul.
