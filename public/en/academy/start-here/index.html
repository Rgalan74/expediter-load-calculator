The mistake that costs the most money (and how to avoid it today)

01The mistake

Almost everyone calculates RPM by dividing the pay by the loaded miles. But to earn that pay you also drive empty to the pickup and, often, empty after delivery to find the next load.

Pay ÷ loaded miles
Pay ÷ every mile you drive (empty + loaded + repositioning)

02An example with real miles

You are in Toledo, OH. You are offered a pickup in Detroit (59 empty miles) and a delivery in Oklahoma City (1,027 loaded miles) for $1,500. Afterwards you move to Dallas to find freight (206 miles).

04Five checks before accepting

  • Every mile. Enter in the calculator the empty miles to the pickup and after, if the delivery leaves you without freight.
  • Your real cost. Fill in Settings once: van, insurance, fuel, maintenance and your margin.
  • The verdict. The calculator says ACCEPT, EVALUATE or REJECT with your numbers, and how much to ask if it falls short.
  • Where it leaves you. Check in Zones whether you get out of that state fast or end up sitting.
  • Tolls and notes. The app warns you if the route has tolls and reminds you of what you noted about that place.
