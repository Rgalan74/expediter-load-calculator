🗺️ BEFORE MODULE 1

App Tour

Get to know every screen in Smart Load Solution in under 15 minutes, before diving into the business theory.

⏱️ 10-15 minutes 🟢 Very Easy 📖 6 screens

🎯 What this is

This isn't a business-theory module — the rest of the Academy covers that. This is a quick walkthrough of the app: where everything lives and what it's for, so you're not hunting around blind while you learn to use it in depth in the modules that follow.

📱 The 6 Screens

SCREEN 1

📊 Calculator

The screen you'll live in most of the time. Enter origin, destination, miles, and rate — it tells you your real RPM, your profit, and whether the load is worth it. It's the heart of the app; all of Module 2 is dedicated to mastering it.

📊
SCREEN 2

📋 History — your Trip Manager

The complete record of every load: origin, destination, date, RPM. You can filter by origin, destination, year, or month. It's your trip history — and the data source Lex AI uses to know your real performance.

📋
SCREEN 3

💰 Finances — your Dashboard

Full financial control: Summary (charts of income, expenses, and profit by period), Reports, and Accounts (your operating expenses). This is where you see at a glance how your business is doing this month vs last month.

💰
SCREEN 4

🗺️ Zones — Zone Map

An interactive USA map. Hover over a state and see how it's worked out for you: your average RPM, how many loads you've run there. It helps you spot your strong zones — and the ones worth avoiding — at a glance.

🗺️
SCREEN 5

🧠 Lex AI

Your AI assistant, available as a chat from anywhere in the app. Ask it about the load on your screen, your finances this month, or how a state has worked out for you — it uses your real data, not generic answers. We go deeper in Module 2, Lesson 6.

🧠
SCREEN 6

⚙️ Settings — Profile & Preferences

Configure your real costs (fuel, maintenance, fixed expenses) and your target margin. These numbers are the foundation of every calculation the app makes — worth getting right from day one.

⚙️

🚀 Ready for the real content

Now you know where everything lives. Next comes the part that actually changes your business: the mindset and the numbers behind every decision.
