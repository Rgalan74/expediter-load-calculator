Module 8 · Lesson 1

Spotting burnout in time

8 min read Wellbeing Premium
What you will learn
  1. The warning signs of burnout.
  2. How to prevent it every day, week and month.
  3. Why to let the numbers decide when you are tired.

01Burnout arrives slowly

Nobody burns out in a day. It is weeks of little sleep, bad food, time away from home and accepting any load. The dangerous part is that when you notice it, it is already affecting your health, your family and your business.

02Warning signs

In your bodyTiredness that sleep does not fix, frequent headaches or back pain, getting sick more often
In your moodIrritable with brokers or family, nothing excites you, you feel you work without moving forward
In your behaviorYou isolate, sleep badly, eat or drink too much, get distracted while driving
In your businessYou accept loads below Break-even to avoid negotiating, forget to record loads and expenses, make costly mistakes
Behind the wheel

If you struggle to keep your eyes open or cannot remember the last miles, stop and rest. No load is worth a crash.

03Prevention on three levels

Every daySleep at similar hours, eat real food, move at every stop, talk to someone at home
Every weekOne full day without work, planned ahead
Every monthReview your numbers and your energy. If profit goes up but you feel worse, something has to change

04Let the numbers decide

When you are tired, you decide worse. The calculator decides the same at any hour: if a load says REJECT or EVALUATE, do not take it just out of tiredness or fear of sitting idle.

If you are in crisis

If you are thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7, English and Spanish). You can also text HOME to 741741. If there is immediate danger, call 911.

In summary

  • Burnout arrives slowly and hurts your business.
  • Prevent it with daily habits, a day off and a monthly review.
  • When tired, let the calculator decide.
