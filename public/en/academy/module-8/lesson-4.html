Module 8 · Lesson 4

Mental health and resilience

8 min read Wellbeing Premium
What you will learn
  1. Why life on the road weighs on you.
  2. Six simple strategies to look after yourself.
  3. When and where to ask for help.

01The part nobody sees

Days alone on the road, pressure on every load, money uncertainty and time away from family. It is normal for it to weigh on you. Looking after your mind is part of the job, just like checking the tires.

02Six strategies

ConnectionTalk every day with someone you care about, even briefly
RoutineSimilar times to sleep, eat and call give stability when everything else changes
Control what you canYou do not decide the market; you do decide which loads you take. Your numbers give you that calm
BreaksA few minutes of slow breathing or silence at a stop lower the tension
Less noiseLimit news and social media when they put you in a bad mood
GratitudeBefore sleeping, write down three things that went well that day

03Asking for help takes strength

If you have been sad for weeks, not sleeping, not wanting to do anything or with anxiety that does not go away, talk to a professional. Many health plans cover online visits you can do from the cab.

If you are in crisis

If you are thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7, English and Spanish). You can also text HOME to 741741. If there is immediate danger, call 911.

04Bad days will come

A cancelled load, a broker who does not pay, a slow week. Resilience is not never falling: it is having a plan to get back up. Your cash reserves, your trusted brokers and your habits are that plan.

In summary

  • Looking after your mind is part of the job.
  • Connection, routine and controlling what depends on you.
  • Asking for help takes strength; in a crisis, 988.
