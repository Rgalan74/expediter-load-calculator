Module 8 · Lesson 3

Physical health on the road

8 min read Wellbeing Premium
What you will learn
  1. The five pillars of your health on the road.
  2. How to eat better and spend less.
  3. How to add habits without getting overwhelmed.

01Your body is your main tool

You look after the van because without it there is no business. The same goes for you: hours sitting, fast food and irregular sleep take their toll over the years. Small, steady habits make the difference.

02Five pillars

SleepAdults need at least 7 hours. Darken the cab, keep similar hours and no screens right before sleeping
FoodCarry a cooler with prepared food, fruit and water. You save money and eat better than at the truck stop
MovementWalk and stretch at every stop. Ten minutes a day already helps your back and circulation
WaterKeep a bottle at hand. Thirst is also mistaken for tiredness
Check-upsA medical check-up every year: blood pressure, sugar, eyesight. Better to catch things early

03It is also money

Record food and hotels in Finances → Add expense (Food, Lodging). Lodging goes into your cost per mile; food is a personal expense and does not, but this way you see what eating out costs you and how much you save by bringing your food.

One change at a time

Pick a single habit, for example walking 10 minutes at every stop, and keep it for two weeks before adding another.

Important

This lesson is general information, not medical advice. For any symptom or question, see a health professional.

In summary

  • Sleep, food, movement, water and check-ups.
  • Bringing your food is healthier and cheaper.
  • One habit at a time.
