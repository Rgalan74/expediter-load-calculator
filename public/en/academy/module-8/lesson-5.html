01Thinking in decades

Expediting can give you a good life, but your body does not drive forever. What you do today with your profit decides how you will be in 10, 20 or 30 years.

02The basics

Emergency fund3 to 6 months of business and household fixed costs, kept separate
Health insuranceAs an independent, nobody gives it to you: include it in your costs
RetirementAccounts like a SEP-IRA, Solo 401(k) or Roth IRA. Which fits you depends on your case: ask your accountant
Taxes up to dateSet money aside every month for estimated payments. The app's tax report helps your accountant

03Time works for you

Saving a little but steadily, for many years, adds up to more than it seems. Returns are not guaranteed: use this calculator only to get an idea.

04Beyond money

  • Family: let your work give them time with you, not just money.
  • Health: reach 60 driving because you want to, not because you have to.
  • Community: teach beginners what you learned.
  • Options: an orderly business lets you choose: keep going, grow or sell.
