Module 8 · Lesson 2

Practical balance: planning the week

8 min read Wellbeing Premium
What you will learn
  1. How to calculate how many days you need to work.
  2. The four areas worth looking after.
  3. Simple rules to protect your time at home.

01Balance is planned

Expediting has no fixed schedule, so balance does not happen by itself. You plan it, just like a route. The useful question is not "how much can I work?" but "how many days do I need to work to reach my goal?".

02How many days you need

With your weekly profit goal and your average profit per day (you see it load by load in the panel), you know how many days you have to work.

Days you need—
Possible days off—
The lever

Raising your profit per day with better loads (modules 2 to 4) is what gives you days at home back. Choosing by profit per day, not total pay, is also choosing free time.

03Four areas to look after

Family and partnerShare your weekly plan. One set call every day is worth more than many rushed ones
HealthSleep, food and movement (lesson 3)
RestAt least one full day a week with no loads or work calls
YouSomething you enjoy that has nothing to do with the truck

04Rules that help

  • Decide your home days before the week starts and tell your brokers.
  • Look for the week's last load with a destination near home.
  • On your day off, the work phone stays on silent.
  • If you overworked one week, make up for it the next.

In summary

  • Balance is planned like a route.
  • Weekly goal ÷ profit per day = days you need.
  • Raising your profit per day gives you days at home back.
