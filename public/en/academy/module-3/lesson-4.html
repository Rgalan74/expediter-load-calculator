Strategic empty miles: when repositioning pays

8 min read Strategy Free
What you will learn
  1. When driving empty is an investment.
  2. How to compare staying or moving.
  3. How to do it with the app calculator.

01Empty miles as an investment

Driving empty is not always losing money. Sometimes it is the cheapest way to get out of a bad zone and into one with good freight. The question is simple:

Profit of the load there − cost of the empty miles > profit of the best load here?

02Case: you delivered in Laredo

Cost of $0.55 per mile. The best offer in Laredo goes to Dallas: 430 miles for $258 ($0.60 per mile). In San Antonio, 157 miles away, there is one to Atlanta: 988 miles for $1,000.

The 157 empty miles cost $86. In return you make $349 more, about $185 per day instead of $21, and you end in a green state.

In the calculator

You do not need to do the math by hand: quote San Antonio → Atlanta and put the 157 miles in Empty miles. The panel already gives you the real profit of moving.

04When it is not worth it

  • The load there is not confirmed: moving without a booked load is a gamble.
  • The empty run is so long it eats up the difference.
  • There is an acceptable load here that leaves you in a good zone.
Your home radius

Many drivers set an empty-mile limit, for example 150 miles, unless the math above clearly says otherwise. That way empty miles are a decision, not a habit.

In summary

  • Move if the load there minus the empty miles leaves more than the best one here.
  • Also count the days and where you end up.
  • In the app, put the empty miles in Empty miles and the panel does the math.
