How to use the Zones tab

8 min read Strategy Free
What you will learn
  1. What each part of Zones shows.
  2. How to read the state table.
  3. How to save market notes and where they show up again.

01What the Zones tab has

Heat mapEach state in green, yellow or red. Hover to see its data
State zone tableLoads, RPM, profit and level of each state
Destination cities mapThe cities where you delivered, with their RPM, and the most frequent ones
Market notesWhat you learned about each place, so you do not forget it

02The state table

LoadsHow many loads you took leaving that state
RPMThe average of those loads
ProfitThe net profit of those loads added up
Score and performanceThe level as a number: 75 green, 37 yellow, 10 red

Tap a column header to sort. Print report gives you a version to save or share.

03Destination cities

Use it to see which cities your brokers send you to and which ones pay best. If a city keeps coming up and pays little, that is a conversation to have with that broker.

04Market notes

Type the place, choose whether the note applies when it is the destination, the origin or both, and write what you learned: long waits, difficult docks, little outbound freight, a broker who pays late.

Where they show up again

When you quote a load to that place, the note appears in Keep in mind, below the panel. Lex also uses it to back up a counter-offer.

0510-minute monthly routine

  • Check which states changed color.
  • Sort the table by profit: are your best states the ones you visit most?
  • Review the cities that keep coming up and pay little.
  • Write a note for every place that gave you trouble this month.

In summary

  • The table sums up outbound loads, RPM and profit by state.
  • The cities map shows where your loads end and how much they pay.
  • Notes show up in Keep in mind and Lex uses them.
