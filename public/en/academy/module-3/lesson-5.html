Chain thinking: the 4 required questions

Before you accept absolutely ANY load, ask yourself:

  1. Exactly where does this drop me off? What is the Zone Score? What is the expected wait time?
  2. What are my realistic options from there? Are there multiple high-quality outbound lanes? Or am I effectively trapped?
  3. Does this drive me TOWARDS my home circuit or PULL ME AWAY from it? Drawing me into profitability? Or pulling me toward a desert?
  4. What is my honest 3-load chain projection? Total net profit? Daily average profit?
How to decide
4/4 positiveAccept instantly
3/4 positiveProbably accept
2/4 positiveNegotiate fiercely or pause to analyze
≤1 positiveAutomatically decline

Accept the "mediocre" Louisville → Detroit run, BECAUSE it specifically transports you into a premium profit zone.

  • 3-load chain: Flawless execution ✅✅

Major red flags in chain planning

Imminent warning signs — hit 3+ and completely reconsider your plan
The entire chain drags you >800 miles away from your established home circuit
ANY expected drop-off zone in the chain has a score < 60
Multiple legs project wait times exceeding 24 hours
Your daily average net profit dips below $200
There is absolutely no clear "return path" to favorable zones
You realize you're blindly chasing "one massive payload" directly into a dead zone
The survival of your profit margin relies entirely on "hoping and praying" for a good backhaul
