The 5 critical factors

What makes a zone "profitable"?

  1. Freight density = Number of available loads per day
  2. Load-to-truck ratio = More loads than trucks = better for you
  3. Average wait time = How long it takes to get your next load
  4. Rate quality = Typical RPM for loads in that zone
  5. Backhaul availability = Probability of finding a return load

How to identify your gold zones

Method 1: Analysis in Smart Load Solution

Dashboard → Zones → My Top Zones

The app analyzes your history and automatically shows:

  • 🥇 Top 5 zones with best performance
  • Avg RPM per zone
  • Avg wait time
  • Success rate
  • ⚠️ Bottom 3 zones to avoid

Method 2: The 4-question test

For any zone:

  1. How long did I wait for my last load here?
    <12 hrs=🟢 | 12-24 hrs=🟡 | >24 hrs = 🔴
  2. What RPM did I average here?
    >$1.50 = 🟢 | $1.30-1.50 = 🟡 | <$1.30=🔴
  3. Did I find a backhaul easily?
    Yes = 🟢 | Sometimes = 🟡 | No = 🔴
  4. Do I have reliable contacts/brokers here?
    Yes = 🟢 | Some = 🟡 | No = 🔴
Scoring

The "Home Circuit" concept

Instead of roaming all over the US randomly, build a CIRCUIT of 4-6 premium zones.

90-day plan

Weeks 1-4: Discovery

  • Track ALL your zones in the app
  • Note wait times, RPM, ease of reload
  • Identify your top 3 best performers

Weeks 5-8: Focus

  • Spend 60% of your time in your top 3 zones
  • Develop contacts with local brokers
  • Learn the area (parking, fuel, etc.)

Weeks 9-12: Optimization

  • Refine your circuit (add 2-3 more zones)
  • Establish "no-go zones"
  • Compare profit month 1 vs month 3
