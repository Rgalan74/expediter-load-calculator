How it works

From the broker's offer to a decision backed by numbers

Three steps. The first time takes 5 minutes; after that, each load takes 30 seconds.

1

Set your costs once

Van payment, insurance, maintenance and the miles you run per month. The app works out your real cost per mile.

2

Type or paste the load

Origin, destination and the rate. The app gets the miles, the fuel price on that route and the tolls.

3

Decide with numbers

It tells you Accept, Evaluate or Reject, what you keep net, and what to ask for if you counter-offer.

Who built it

I built it because I've been there too

I spent a year driving my cargo van all over the United States. My home base was Florida, but I only made it home to rest every two or three months.

I looked for an app that would help me run the business, or at least teach me how, and I couldn't find one. So I started building it myself.

I fed it every day with what I lived on each trip: the loads that weren't worth it, the costs I didn't see coming, and what actually worked.

🚐 1 year on the road🗺️ All over the U.S.🏠 Based in Florida

FAQ

What people ask most

Is it free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free, with no time limit and no card: 30 loads a month, the calculator, basic finances, the zone map and the first 3 Academy modules. Every new account also gets 14 days of Lex AI free.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in your phone's and computer's browser with the same account. Nothing to install.

Where do the costs come from?

From what you enter in Settings: van payment, insurance, maintenance and monthly miles. Fuel uses the average price along the route (EIA data) and your van's MPG.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts. You cancel from your account, and paid plans have a 14-day guarantee.
