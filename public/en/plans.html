FAQ

Before you choose

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move from Starter to Professional or Premium anytime and the change is immediate. If you already pay, you switch or cancel from your account.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts. You cancel from your account and keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. Paid plans have a 14-day guarantee from your first payment. Details are in the refund policy.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Do I need a card for the free plan?

No. Create your account and start using it. The Starter plan asks for no payment method and never expires.

What happens when the 14 days of Lex AI end?

If you stay on Starter, Lex AI pauses; everything else stays the same and you are not charged. Professional gives you 10 Lex messages a day and Premium + AI gives you 150.

What does the tax report include?

A summary of your income, deductible expenses and miles for the year, laid out like Schedule C, to take to your accountant.
