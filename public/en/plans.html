❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade from Starter to Pro or Premium at any time from your settings panel. The change is immediate.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no long-term contracts. Cancel anytime and your access continues until the end of your billing period.

Is payment secure?

Absolutely. All payments are processed through Stripe, the world leader in online payments. We never store your card details.

What's included in the tax report?

A detailed summary of all your deductible expenses, miles driven, and net earnings formatted to simplify your annual tax filing.

Do I need a card for the free plan?

No. The Starter Plan requires no payment method. Just create your account and start using it immediately.

What happens when the Lex AI trial ends?

After 14 days your account stays on Starter. Lex AI locks until you upgrade to Premium + AI — no charges, no surprises.
