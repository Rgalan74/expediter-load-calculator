❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card for the free plan?

+
No. The free plan gives you 30 loads per month without needing to enter any payment method. Just create your account and start using it immediately.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

+
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your settings panel. No contracts or penalties. Once cancelled, you keep access until the end of the already paid period.

Is my data safe?

+
Yes. We use Firebase (Google Cloud), which encrypts your data in transit and at rest. Your financial data is private and we never sell it or share it with third parties.

Does it work on my phone?

+
Yes. Smart Load Solution is a 100% responsive Progressive Web App (PWA). It works on any device: iPhone, Android, tablet, or desktop computer.

What happens if I exceed the 30 free loads?

+
You can still see your history and dashboard, but to save more loads that month you need the Professional plan. The counter resets at the start of each month.

Can I export my data to Excel?

+
Yes. With Professional or Premium you can export to Excel or CSV from your history and from Finances.
