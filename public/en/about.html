Our Mission: Empowering Independent Carriers

Our mission is to empower independent carriers with smart tools to maximize their profitability and make informed decisions.

🚚 Our Story

Smart Load Solution was born on the road. I spent a year driving my cargo van all over the United States, based in Florida and only making it home to rest every two or three months. Every day I had to decide which loads to take without really knowing what I would keep.

I looked for an app that would help me run the business, or at least teach me how, and I couldn't find one. So I started building it myself, and fed it every day with what I lived on each trip.

Today Smart Load Solution brings all of that together: the real RPM calculator, business finances, the zone map, Lex AI and an academy, so you don't have to learn it the hard way like I did. — Ricardo Galán
