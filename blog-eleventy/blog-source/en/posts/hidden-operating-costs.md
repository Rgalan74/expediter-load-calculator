<h2 class="text-xl md:text-2xl font-bold text-white mt-8 mb-4">🔍 The Illusion of Visible Costs</h2>
<p>The classic mistake of beginner owner-operators is thinking that <strong>fuel</strong> and <strong>tolls</strong>
  are their only operating costs. But in practice, those visible expenses usually represent only part of the
  real cost of keeping a cargo van working every day.</p>

<div class="callout warning">
  <strong class="text-orange-400 font-bold">❌ The Danger of Ignoring Hidden Costs</strong>
  <p>When you don't calculate your monthly fixed costs per mile or account for the real wear on your vehicle,
    you can end up accepting loads that look good but actually lose you money. In short: you're trading your
    van's future value for immediate cash flow.</p>
</div>

<h2 class="text-xl md:text-2xl font-bold text-white mt-8 mb-4">👻 The Three Most Aggressive Silent Costs</h2>

<h3 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-semibold text-white mt-6 mb-2">1. Vehicle Depreciation ($0.05 - $0.08/mi)</h3>
<p>This is one of the most invisible expenses because it doesn't come out of your pocket every day. Consider
  this example:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-6 space-y-1 my-3 text-sm md:text-base">
  <li>You buy a new van for <strong>$80,000</strong>.</li>
  <li>In 5 years, after driving 400,000 miles, its resale value drops to <strong>$30,000</strong>.</li>
  <li>You lost <strong>$50,000</strong> in asset value, which equals <strong>$0.125 per mile</strong> in pure
    depreciation.</li>
</ul>
<p>You don't see that loss every time you fill up the tank, but when it's time to replace the van, the financial
  hit arrives all at once.</p>

<h3 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-semibold text-white mt-6 mb-2">2. The Van Payment as a Cost Per Mile</h3>
<p>Your van payment is a fixed monthly cost. For example, if you pay <strong>$1,800 a month</strong>, its real
  weight per mile depends on how many productive miles you run:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-6 space-y-1 my-3 text-sm md:text-base">
  <li>If you run <strong>6,000 miles</strong> a month: the payment equals <strong>$0.30 per mile</strong>
    ($1,800 ÷ 6,000).</li>
  <li>If you run <strong>10,000 miles</strong> a month: the cost drops to <strong>$0.18 per mile</strong>
    ($1,800 ÷ 10,000).</li>
</ul>
<p>Running more profitable miles helps dilute your fixed costs and makes your operation far more competitive.</p>

<h3 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-semibold text-white mt-6 mb-2">3. Commercial Insurance Cost</h3>
<p>Commercial liability and cargo insurance can cost around <strong>$10,500 a year</strong> ($875 a month).
  <strong>You pay it whether you drive or not.</strong> If your van is parked for a week, insurance keeps running
  and your daily break-even point goes up.</p>

<div class="callout tip">
  <strong class="text-cyan-400">💡 The Golden Rule of the Smart Expediter</strong>
  <p>When your van is on the road, it consumes variable costs: fuel, wear, maintenance. When it's parked, it
    keeps consuming fixed costs: insurance, payment, permits and apps. <strong>There are no expense-free
      days.</strong> Knowing your daily fixed cost is the first step to protecting your profitability.</p>
</div>
